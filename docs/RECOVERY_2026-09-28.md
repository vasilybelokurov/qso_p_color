# Legacy PSF recovery: fixes and validation, 28 September 2026

The Legacy PSF baseline has recovered the intended relative-flux design and
improves broad candidate ranking. Its complete release validation **fails**.
The scoring interface now excludes the reproduced unsafe cases, while the
remaining failures are recorded rather than hidden behind the earlier PASS.
No density artifact or `current` pointer was changed by this recovery pass.
The subsequent spatial-background restoration now changes `current`; the
commands below use the immutable pooled bundle to reproduce this report.

## Which model and which claim

Bundle `8e2a27c013ed`, loaded by `XDQSOBaseline`, uses dereddened Legacy DR9
relative fluxes, quasar colour slices independent of apparent magnitude, and
field mixtures in r bins. It is an experimental ranking baseline for PSF
companions with 17 <= r < 22.5 and |b| >= 25 degrees. The north remains
provisional. The older 41-band model remains available through `MultiSurveyModel`;
its native-flux convention and its separate limitations still apply.

The README and AGENTS previously called the multi-survey release the only
model. The method note described that release while the newer baseline was
published separately. These instructions now distinguish the two routes.

## Repairs

- `XDQSOBaseline.score_rows` requires an explicit excluding `BlendPolicy`
  with finite separation and fracflux limits. Missing measurements exclude
  the companion. A flag-only policy cannot certify a science candidate.
- The caller supplies a positive finite `ood_flag_sigma`. Beyond that distance
  from **both** populations, using all redshifts for the quasar distance, the
  interface returns `status='outside_both_models'`, retains diagnostic
  likelihoods, intensities and distances, and withholds all posterior
  probabilities and `log_r_per_unit_z`. The declared validation threshold is 4.
  This is an explicit support selection, not a fit or calibration of eta.
- Outputs carry the bundle identifier; default row identifiers remain unique
  across hemispheres. Component selection identifiers are checked on load.
- Direct tests exercise the actual published XDQSO bundle, including the
  southern and northern tail counterexamples, missing blending information,
  provenance, and the dereddened adapter against an explicit Jacobian.
- Future `train_qso_model.py --morphology point` runs require the complete local
  store and use the baseline selection, hemisphere rule, latitude restriction,
  and per-band nobs masks. They include southern releases 9010 and 9012.
  This change does **not** retroactively alter the published training sample:
  its southern quasar model used release 9010 only and included lower latitudes.

The reproduced bright southern counterexample was 8.35 sigma from every quasar
component and 22.52 sigma from the field, yet had total quasar posterior 1 and
ln R = 1.98. Its Gaussian outlier share was effectively zero. The guarded
interface now excludes that row. It also excludes the northern counterexamples.
The underlying density fit remains unchanged; raw model intensities outside
support must not be converted back into science probabilities.

The saved paired spatial bootstrap supports the ranking and star-rejection

negatives just outside the velocity window remain indistinguishable. These
sky blocks were excluded from fitting, but have been inspected during several
rounds of model development; this is not a new external confirmation sample.

### Field counts and tails

The check uses all 97,984 selected sources in 39 reserved southern cones over
15 cells, before the support exclusion. The simulated observable population
contains non-quasars, unmodelled objects, unrecognised quasars under their
adopted surface-density priors, measurement noise with full covariance, and
missing bands. It is conditional on each object's measured r and error matrix.
Known quasars are removed from the observations and accounted for by the
recognised fraction in the simulation.

| Nearest field-component distance | Observed | Expected, five simulations |
|---|---:|---:|
| 4-6 sigma | 121 | 167.4 |
| 6-10 sigma | 38 | 99.0 |
| >= 10 sigma | 25 | 115.8 |

The last two bins fail the predeclared test: discrepancy exceeding a factor of
two and three standard deviations, with at least 20 expected. Almost all the
predicted objects in those bins are **unrecognised quasars**, not the broad
Gaussian term. This diagnoses a problem in the observable-population model;
it does not justify broadening the non-quasar density or adjusting eta to
force a pass on these test cones. Counts are 1.28 times the global prediction,
with a broad spatial-bootstrap interval of approximately 0.84-1.99.
There are no reserved northern field cones, so northern tails are untested.

### Continuation and score stability

`check_xdqso_continuation.py` explicitly replays the historical quasar selection,
including its release-9010 restriction. It verifies the total training and
holdout counts and the membership count of every slice before continuing all
86 saved mixtures for 200 iterations. Training totals match: 1,023,844 south
and 455,394 north; held-out totals are 309,150 and 8,635 respectively.
The diagnostic fits are kept outside the published bundle.

| Check | Measurement | Declared limit | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Southern quasar slices: largest absolute held-out mean density change | 0.00533 nat/object | 0.01 | pass |
| Northern quasar slices: largest absolute held-out mean density change | 0.03342 nat/object | 0.01 | fail |
| Field bins: largest absolute select-density change | 0.00085 nat/object | 0.01 | pass |
| Median absolute ln R change after field continuation | 0.00653 | 0.02 | pass |
| Median absolute ln R change after quasar continuation | 0.02806 | 0.02 | fail |

The quasar score-change 95th percentile is 0.332. A small mean density change
alone therefore does not establish stable individual rankings. The numerical
missing-band check and the prior-normalisation identity still pass.

Field continuation uses the exact original sampled rows and seed. The
original final-fit responsibility weights were not saved, so they are refreshed
at the published model. This is a fixed-point stability check, not an exact
recovery of the preceding outer iteration's objective. The north's original
contamination iteration also stopped above its configured convergence tolerance.

## Reproduction

Run from the repository root, with the existing local catalogue store and
field-cone caches. These checks make no database queries. Diagnostic output
locations are explicit so the immutable bundle remains untouched.

```bash
python scripts/check_xdqso_continuation.py \
  --bundle models/legacy_psf_xdqso/8e2a27c013ed \
  --output /tmp/qso-continuation --workers 4
python scripts/validate_xdqso_baseline.py \
  --bundle models/legacy_psf_xdqso/8e2a27c013ed \
  --quasar-continuation /tmp/qso-continuation \
  --continuation-cache /tmp/qso-field-continuation \
  --output docs/VALIDATION_legacy_psf_xdqso_recovery.json
```

The second command exits nonzero for this bundle. Omitting the quasar
continuation also fails validation; missing checks cannot produce PASS.
Tail definitions and acceptance limits are recorded in
`configs/legacy_baseline.json`, alongside the explicit science-support policy.

Software verification: the complete regression suite passes, **234 tests** in
57.85 seconds. The README's Legacy scoring example runs against the published
bundle. The method PDF was rebuilt and its updated opening pages inspected.
Passing these software checks does not change the failed scientific verdict.

## Remaining scientific work

A fully validated successor needs a stable quasar fit and a defensible
observable-field prediction. The planned comparison with high spectroscopic
coverage cones remains the useful independent test of the unrecognised-quasar
correction. That comparison should use fit/select/calibration data, with new
reserved regions for confirmation after any model choice. Northern validation
requires reserved northern field cells and refitting without them.

These failures warrant a measured follow-up, not another expansion of the
model architecture. The current guarded interface is available for exploratory
ranking; the failed full validation must accompany any use of the bundle.
